# Data license

The annotations this project produces are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).

## What this covers

Values computed by this project and published under [`data/`](data/), in
release assets, or in the project's Hugging Face datasets: scores, labels,
tags, types, concept assignments, dates and date windows, lineage edges,
clusters and the tables and figures built from them. This applies to existing
derived data as well as to everything published from the Skill Concepts Atlas
onward.

## What this does not cover

- **Third-party `SKILL.md` text**, whether in the crawl snapshots under
  [`crawls/`](crawls/) or included in a dataset. It stays under its authors'
  own terms.
- **Short excerpts** quoted inside annotations as evidence. They stay under
  their authors' terms too.
- **Data imported from other datasets**, such as GitSkills metadata, which
  keeps its own license and attribution.
- **Code and documents**, which are under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## How to attribute

> Skill Map annotations by Dave Holmes-Kinsella and contributors
> (https://github.com/dhk/skill-map), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

When a table also carries data from another source, credit that source as its
license asks. Each published table names its sources and method version.

See [DATA-POLICY.md](DATA-POLICY.md) for what the project stores and publishes.
